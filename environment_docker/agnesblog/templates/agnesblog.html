Backpacking 101

Essential Gear for Your First Backpacking Trip

Agnes Miller November 11, 2026

Stepping off the paved path and into the backcountry for the first time is a thrilling experience. The air smells cleaner, the silence is profound, and carrying everything you need to survive on your back is incredibly empowering. However, the difference between a life-changing adventure and a miserable slog often comes down to one thing: your gear.

When I started hiking a decade ago, I made the classic mistake of overpacking while under-investing in the "Big Three": your shelter, your sleep system, and your pack. Let's break down exactly what you should be looking for before you hit the trailhead.

1. The Backpack: Your Mobile Home

Don't buy a pack online without getting fitted if you can help it. A 65-liter pack is generally the sweet spot for a beginner embarking on a 2-3 day trip. A high-quality internal frame pack with a good suspension system will distribute weight onto your hips rather than your shoulders. If your shoulders ache after a mile, your pack is adjusted wrong.

2. Shelter: Keep the Elements Out

Your tent is your sanctuary. For a first-timer, a freestanding double-wall tent (meaning it has an inner mesh body and a separate waterproof rainfly) is the most foolproof choice. Look for a reliable, lightweight model with sturdy aluminum poles to balance durability with packability.

3. Footwear: Don't Compromise

Nothing ruins a trip faster than blisters. While many long-distance hikers have shifted to lightweight trail runners, traditional hiking boots still offer superior ankle support, which is crucial when you are carrying 30+ pounds for the first time. Look for boots with good ankle support, deep lugs for traction, and a waterproof membrane if you expect wet conditions. Pair them with merino wool socks (never cotton!) to manage moisture.

Remember, the best gear is the gear you know how to use. Always set up your tent in your living room and break in your boots around the neighborhood before you find yourself ten miles deep in the woods!
